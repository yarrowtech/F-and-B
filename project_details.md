# Project Details

## Project Summary

EFNBMMS is a restaurant operations and business management system designed to help restaurants manage billing, inventory, staff workflows, kitchen coordination, vendor interaction, and reporting from one connected platform. It supports both manual billing restaurants and hybrid restaurants that use manual billing together with the platform's table-based and system-driven billing workflow.

## Features

- Multi-role access for superadmin, admin, manager, accountant, waiter, chef, su chef, cleaner, inventory manager, and vendor
- Restaurant-based access control with support for assigned restaurant visibility
- Manual-only and hybrid restaurant type management
- Table management and live order handling for supported restaurants
- Accountant billing with GST, service charges, discounts, complimentary items, and billing history
- Inventory management with stock updates, low stock alerts, stock value tracking, and stock approvals
- Kitchen section stock transfer and location-wise stock tracking
- Staff management and restaurant assignment controls
- Vendor management, vendor storefront, and supplier interaction workflows
- Admin, manager, and vendor reporting modules
- Public-facing pages including home, services, subscription, contact, privacy policy, and public menu

## Target Audience

- Restaurant owners
- Restaurant administrators
- Branch managers
- Accountants and billing staff
- Waiters and floor staff
- Kitchen teams including chefs and su chefs
- Inventory managers and stock handlers
- Restaurant vendors and suppliers
- Multi-branch or multi-restaurant operators

## Goals

- Centralize restaurant operations in one unified system
- Reduce billing, stock, and workflow errors caused by disconnected manual processes
- Support different restaurant operating styles through manual-only and hybrid billing models
- Provide role-based tools so each team member sees only the functions relevant to their work
- Improve operational visibility through reporting, inventory tracking, and billing records
- Make restaurant management more organized, scalable, and efficient
